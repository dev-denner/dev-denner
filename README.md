## Selected Professional Experience

### Whirlpool Corporation — Post-Purchase Platform

Backend platform whose central API managed order state and supplied customer service channels, chatbots, websites and internal applications.

**Stack:** `Node.js` · `TypeScript` · `NestJS` · `Prisma` · `PostgreSQL` · `DynamoDB` · `AWS Lambda` · `SNS` · `SQS` · `CloudWatch` · `VTEX` · `SAP`

**My role:** Worked on development and technical leadership of the post-purchase platform, participated in order information modeling and API exposure decisions, migrated components from JavaScript to TypeScript, supported other developers, and contributed to the Order Monitor backend.

I also redesigned serverless flows previously distributed across independent Lambda functions into a processing pipeline with clearer responsibilities, reducing coordination complexity.

---

### Zup Innovation — Open Finance

Backend solution developed in the Open Banking initiative, later Open Finance, including the corporate authority and powers flow for legal entities.

**Stack:** `Node.js` · `TypeScript` · `AWS Lambda` · `DynamoDB` · `Serverless`

**My role:** Participated from domain understanding through architecture and implementation, translating Open Banking requirements into backend services and working on a serverless pipeline based on AWS Lambda.

**Documented outcome:** The team was the first within the project context to complete and deliver the solution it had architected.

---

### Terpan — Backend, Cloud & Architecture

Technical restructuring of an operation dependent on white-label solutions and applications distributed across multiple servers.

**Stack:** `AWS` · `APIs` · `Cloud Infrastructure`

**My role:** Mapped systems, integrations and dependencies; designed and implemented the cloud architecture; structured APIs, environments, users, roles and access controls; and conducted the technical assessment of an ERP before its acquisition.

**Documented outcome:** The consolidation reduced the operational complexity of maintaining applications across multiple servers and established a centralized base for maintenance, updates and system evolution.

---

### Esfera Energia — Energy Market Platform

Platform for management of the energy market based on a microservices architecture.

**Stack:** `Node.js` · `TypeScript` · `NestJS` · `TypeORM` · `MySQL` · `AWS` · `Serverless` · `CQRS` · `EventBridge` · `Angular`

**My role:** Developed and evolved backend functionality in microservices, worked with CQRS and EventBridge for asynchronous communication between components, contributed to frontend development with Angular, and participated in the analysis and adoption of Akita for state management.
